# Plann Workflow

Use the `plann` skill to guide the planning process for the user's request. Follow the steps and headers defined in the skill:
1. Formulate the Objective & Service Strategy (ITIL).
2. Analyze Architecture & Impact (ITIL).
3. Identify Bottlenecks & Race Conditions (Six Sigma).
4. Evaluate Failure Modes & Quality Gates (Six Sigma).
5. Generate the Execution Roadmap (Agile).
